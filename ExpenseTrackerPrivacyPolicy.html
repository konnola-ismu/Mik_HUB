Privacy Policy for Expense Tracker

Last Updated: October 2026

Mik HUB built the Expense Tracker app as a Free app. This SERVICE is provided by Mik HUB at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our Service, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. However, Expense Tracker is designed to respect your privacy. All user-entered data is stored entirely locally on your device.

We do not collect, store, or transmit your financial data, expense records, or personal information to any external servers.

2. Device Permissions

To function properly, Expense Tracker requests access to the following device features:

  • Camera and Photo Library: We request access to your device's camera and photo gallery to allow you to attach images (such as receipts) to your expense records. These images remain on your device and are never uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties.
  • Storage / File System: We request storage permissions to allow you to generate, save, and share reports (like PDFs or CSVs) locally on your device.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Expense Tracker does not use any third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics), nor do we serve advertisements. We do not track your behavior across other apps or websites.

Note: If you decide to add crash reporting like Sentry or Crashlytics in the future, you will need to update this section.

4. Data Security and Deletion

Because all data is stored locally via a local database (SQLite) on your device, we have no control over it. The security of your data depends on the security of your device.

Data Deletion: If you wish to delete your data, you can simply delete the Expense Tracker app from your device. Please note that uninstalling the app will permanently erase all your expense data, and we cannot recover it for you. We recommend exporting your data before uninstalling if you wish to keep a copy.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
