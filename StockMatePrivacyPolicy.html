Privacy Policy for StockMate

Last Updated: October 2026

Mik HUB ("we", "us", or "our") provides the StockMate application (the "App") to help users manage inventory and perform stock takes. This policy explains what information the App handles and the choices available to you.

1. Information the App Handles

StockMate may handle information you enter to use its inventory features, such as product names, descriptions, barcodes, quantities, stock counts, locations, notes, and related images or files. You may also provide information if you contact us for support.

Inventory information is stored on your device. We do not collect or transmit your inventory records or personal information to our own servers. If a version of the App offers an online, backup, or sharing feature, information you choose to submit through that feature may be processed by the service involved, as explained when you use it.

2. Device Permissions

The App may request access to device features when needed for an action you choose:

  • Camera: If barcode scanning or taking product photos is available, camera access is used for that feature. Images and scan results are used in the App and are not sent to our servers.
  • Photos or files: If you choose to attach an existing image or export a stock report, the App may need access to selected photos or files or permission to save the export.

You can manage or revoke permissions in your device settings. Features that depend on a permission may not work if access is denied.

3. How Information Is Used

Information entered into StockMate is used to provide the App's inventory, stock-taking, and reporting features. Information you send to us is used to respond to your request and provide support.

4. Third-Party Services

StockMate does not use third-party advertising or analytics services to track your activity. When you choose to share or export information using another app or service on your device, that service handles the information under its own privacy policy. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your inventory data remains on your device until you delete it or remove the App. Uninstalling StockMate may permanently delete locally stored data. We cannot recover data that has been deleted from your device. Export or back up information you want to keep before deleting records or uninstalling the App.

6. Data Security

Local data is subject to the security of your device and its operating system. Keep your device secure and use any available device lock or backup controls. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

StockMate is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised update date. Please review this page periodically.
