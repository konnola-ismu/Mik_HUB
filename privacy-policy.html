You may choose not to provide certain information; however, this may limit your ability to contact us or use some website features. If you want to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, please use the contact form on this website or contact us via our Instagram page.

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we collect, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please take care to protect your own personal information when using the internet.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated date so you can stay informed about how we handle your information.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please use the contact form on our website or reach out to us through our Instagram page. We are happy to help.
